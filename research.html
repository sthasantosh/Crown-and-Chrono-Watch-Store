Research Page

Before developing our watch e-commerce website, we explored several well-established platforms to understand the standards and features that enhance usability, customer satisfaction, and functionality. This research helped us design a site that is user-friendly, visually appealing, and technologically sound.

Amazon.com

Its strengths are Extensive watch variety, trusted payment system, and detailed customer reviews. And its eakness are Overwhelming layout with too many distractions. After lookin there i learned and made Clean product listing with filters and visible star ratings to make shopping decisions easier.

Chrono24

Its strength are Global marketplace for new and pre-owned watches, advanced search filters. And its weakness are Complex for new users, as it's more marketplace-based. After vsiiting website i obsered and made Simplified browsing experience and clean filtering to prevent confusion for beginners.

Rolex

Its strength are High-end visuals, luxurious brand presentation, minimalistic interface. Its weakness are Limited e-commerce functionality more focused on branding than direct sales. And i adopted Aesthetic balance of elegance and function through high-resolution images and well-structured layout.

Jacob & Co

Its strength are Modern UI, discount visibility, responsive layout, and real-time support. and its weakness are Limited e-commerce functionality; more focused on branding than direct sales. And after observation i adopted Aesthetic balance of elegance and function through high-resolution images and well-structured layout.
